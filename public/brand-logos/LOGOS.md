# Brand logos (Bazaar brand rail)

Official logo files from Wikimedia Commons, self-hosted as SVG. They are optimised with svgo 3 (multipass, preset-default with viewBox kept, width/height removed); the artwork itself is unchanged.
Commons lists each one as **public domain** (a simple text/shape logo below the threshold of originality). All of them are **registered trademarks** of their owners. They are shown only to identify the brand a customer can search for.
R&L is not a partner of these brands, and the Bazaar page says so.
